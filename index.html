Privacy Policy

Yangi O‘zbek Alifbosi · Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yangi O‘zbek Alifbosi (the “App”), an Android keyboard and alphabet-conversion application, handles information when you use it.

Information you type

The App provides an on-device keyboard. Text entered through the keyboard is processed by Android and the app in order to provide typing features. The developer does not receive the text you type through the App. Text entered into another app is also subject to that app’s own privacy practices. As with any third-party keyboard, do not use it to enter sensitive information unless you are comfortable with the keyboard and the receiving app.

Local app data

The App may store certain preferences and convenience information on your device, such as keyboard settings, learned words, recently used emojis, and emoji skin-tone selections. This information is used to provide and personalize the App’s features. It is stored locally and is not sent to the developer. You can remove local app data by clearing the App’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the App. Removing the App may not remove data separately saved by Android or other apps.

Clipboard

If you use a feature that reads clipboard content, the App accesses the clipboard on your device to provide that feature. Clipboard content is processed locally for the requested action and is not sent to the developer.

Documents and text conversion

When you select a TXT, DOCX, or text-based PDF file for conversion, the App processes the selected file on your device to provide the requested conversion. The App does not upload the selected document to the developer. You choose which files to open. Scanned PDFs that contain only images may not be readable as text.

Internet, advertising, analytics, and sharing

The App’s keyboard and text-conversion features are designed to work on your device without requiring an internet connection. The App does not include advertising. The developer does not sell or share your typed text, clipboard content, or selected documents. If a future version adds online services, analytics, advertising, or other third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those practices are introduced.

Permissions and Android keyboard controls

Android may show system notices when you enable or select a third-party keyboard. These notices are controlled by Android and explain the access that keyboard apps can have to text fields. You can enable, disable, or change your selected keyboard at any time in Android settings. Installing this App does not require you to delete your other keyboards.

Children’s privacy

The App does not ask users to provide personal information to the developer. If you believe that personal information has been provided to the developer in connection with the App, contact us using the address below so that the matter can be reviewed.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page, together with its updated date.
